YOUTUBE JOURNEY

My YouTube Journey

It was the time when the lockdown had finally ended, and my classes were about to start. But before that, I had already started a YouTube channel.

In the beginning, I used to upload two videos every day. Later, when my classes started, I continued with one video a day.

Throughout this journey, I learned a lot like how YouTube actually works, how to edit videos, write scripts, and understand the algorithm. But more than anything, I learned patience and consistency.

Slowly after 6-7 months, one of my videos went viral. My channel began to grow, and that moment gave me a big motivation boost.

After months, when my channel finally started growing, it got monetized. I still remember it was a Monday when I received my first income that was around 100 dollars. I know it’s not much, but I’m really proud of it, not just because of the money but because of the skills and experience I gained while earning it.
